Client Supplier

Invoice Financing

Buyer relationships create Dynamic Periods. Funds Requests are submitted from the relevant Dynamic Period.

The Partner Buyer and Counterparty Buyer variants are Financing Relationship models under one product. They are not separate products.

One Dynamic Period groups one Client Supplier, one Buyer, and one invoice Due Date. It recalculates when invoices, Funds Requests, disbursements, Buyer payments, settlements, or approved adjustments change the period.

The requested amount cannot exceed that period's Available to Withdraw. Multiple Funds Requests may be submitted while the request window is open and sufficient availability remains.

Financing Relationships

Buyer relationships

  • Partner Buyer: normally uploads the invoices or receivables information.
  • Counterparty Buyer: normally does not participate; the Client Supplier uploads invoices.
  • Client Supplier: always submits the Funds Request in both relationship models.
