Agri Credit Line

Manage your available credit, Funds Requests, Advances, Instalments, and repayment details in one place.

The credit facility belongs to the financing customer, so Home shows one product-level credit summary, Financing activity, record details, and the Funds Request entry point. Supplier relationships remain inside the relevant commercial transaction rather than appearing as separate facilities.

The Home action and the primary navigation item open the current Agri Credit Line demo in a new tab. That journey collects the financing amount, Instalment option, trade documents, approved disbursement recipient, summary, disclosures, and signature.

The Home card stays deliberately universal: it shows only how many payments need attention and the overdue/upcoming split. Selecting View payments due filters Financing activity to the affected financing periods, where the customer can open the relevant record and its repayment details.

An Agri Credit Line financing customer may purchase eligible goods from a Counterparty Supplier, including a farmer, trader, processor, wholesaler, input supplier, or other approved Supplier. The Supplier may receive the approved disbursement, but it does not become another customer credit facility.

Financing activity

Open a financing period for its full financing and repayment details.

The list is now the place to resolve a payment alert into a specific financing period. View payments due applies a combined due/overdue filter here; selecting a row opens that period, its repayment schedule, and its repayment details.

The Financings.Status superset is broader than what a customer needs on an active financing-period list. Requested, Offered, Validating, Unavailable, pre-signature states, Rolled Over, Refinanced, Repaid, Cancelled, and Declined are excluded here. Customer-facing unsettled states are Live, Overdue, In Default, Delinquent, On Repayment Plan, Transferred to Repayment Plan, and Collections. Overdue can also be derived from the repayment schedule where the source record is otherwise Live.

The product is already fixed to Agri Credit Line, so the controls only need Status, Due Date, and search by financing or Funds Request reference. Desktop uses the space beside the heading; tablet and mobile stack the controls and retain the compact Filters drawer.

The Home payment card does not decide which obligation the customer is paying. It filters the list. Each financing period then carries its own repayment schedule and repayment-details action, so overdue and upcoming obligations cannot be confused with one another.

The old design demonstrated a Purpose column, but the current CRM and LMS dataset does not provide that field. It remains excluded rather than being replaced with invented customer data.
