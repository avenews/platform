 } @case ('abf') {

A Fully Paid Invoice reimburses the Client Buyer and requires Proof of Payment. An Unpaid Invoice directs the approved disbursement to the Supplier. The Financing page demonstrates both paths.

 } @case ('stf') {

The Partner Supplier invoice, delivery date, delivery confirmation, amount, and Instalment choice belong inside the STF Funds Request. Avenews disburses the approved amount to the Partner Supplier.

 } @case ('invoice-financing') {

A Dynamic Period groups one Client Supplier, one Buyer, and one invoice Due Date. The Client Supplier requests funds from that period, subject to its Available to Withdraw and request window.

 } @case ('infx') {

The approved Buyer, invoice, Due Date, POD, and requested amount stay inside one request. The Client Supplier manually repays Avenews at the end of the Financing Period.

 } @case ('invoice-partner') {

The Partner Buyer supplies invoice information and manages the full payment obligation. The Client Supplier remains the financing Client and submits any Funds Request from its Dynamic Period.
