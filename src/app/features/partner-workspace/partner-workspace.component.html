Invoice Financing - Partner Buyer

Invoice Uploads

Upload invoice batches for participating Client Suppliers and review the outcome for every row.

Eligible invoices create or update Dynamic Periods for the relevant Client Supplier. The Client Supplier remains the financing Client and chooses whether to request financing.

The Partner Buyer may upload individual invoices, multiple invoices, a bulk file, or other approved receivables information. Responsibility may be reassigned only when the operational arrangement is clearly documented and duplicate uploads are controlled.
No Funds Request action exists in this workspace. After eligible invoices form a Dynamic Period, the Client Supplier decides whether to request financing.

The current source supports a branded uploader link and form-level feedback. A durable batch record with imported, skipped, and failed row results is not confirmed as a live Partner-facing capability. The table below demonstrates the intended review experience only.
