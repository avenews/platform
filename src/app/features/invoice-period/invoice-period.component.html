Settlement

Buyer payment and settlement

The Buyer pays the full invoice or receivables amount into the designated Client Clearing Account. Avenews allocates the payment, retains the applicable Principal and Markup, and transfers the remaining invoice proceeds to the Client Supplier.

Any approved Partner Buyer rebate is calculated separately and must not appear in the Client Supplier's Available to Withdraw, repayment obligation, or settlement breakdown.

This branch deliberately does not show a bank account number, payment reference, live allocation status, or production settlement instruction because those integrations and final data sources are not established here.
